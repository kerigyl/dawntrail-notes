## Deep Aerial
2 person tower in middle, changes arena to circle. Any 2 players can go in. Healers are recommended. Players are stuck, bosses go untargetable, "Watery Grave" add becomes targetable.

### Parallel Aerial
Start between bosses. Take tethers directly across from bosses, red player shifting directly North or South (whatever's opposite the boss), Blue shades toward intercard. Fire wants to hit the Watery Grave add to cause a raidwide + damage to the add. Blue wants to avoid it.

Everyone can now go to B. Remaining players can now move lines straight across arena. This should do enough damage to the Watery Grave.
