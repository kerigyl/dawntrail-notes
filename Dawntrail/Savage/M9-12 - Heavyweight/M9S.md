# Aetherletting
Pizza Slices spin in a circle. Pre-position in last slice. Player with marker moves out to edge. Dodge into now-safe pizza slice, and another player will get targeted. Continue until all done. The puddles you dropped will then explode into either crosses or X's. Rotate with the explosions as they go off.

## Static Aetherletting
Waymarks show where to stand. You stand on them. They are right on lines between 2 pizza slices. You stand there and dodge sideways as needed to not get hit, but otherwise stay in place. Stand in center and all will be safe.

# Hell In A Cell
2 sets of 4 towers. Group 1 takes first set, Group 2 takes 2nd.

Supports look North and CW. Tank takes first tower, healer takes 2nd.

DPS look NW and CCW. Ranged takes 1st, Melee takes 2nd.

Players are locked in place in a "Charnel Cell." You can only attack your own: you have to break yourself out.

Group 2 pays attention to the boss casting "Ultrasonic Spread" or "Ultrasonic Amp." The first mechanic is random, but always followed by the other.

Spread:
Role Spread
Tank goes to Big Gap, Healers go CW from tank, DPS goes CCW. Spreads go off without clipping caged players.

Amp:
Everyone stack in Big Gap.

HIAC repeats, same rules apply. Dodge ground AOEs.
___
# Undead Deathmatch
Group 1 NW, Group 2 SE. Stack towers appear, move to closest to you. They can spawn N/S or E/W. 2 bats spawned, each player is chained to the bat. Don't stretch too far. Stay close as they move.
Boss casts Sanguine Stretch, alternating AOEs like P4S end mechanic.

Alternate between On and Off waymark. Bats telegraph an AOE when they get back to starting points. North bat does Point-Blank, South Bat does Donut.

Group 1 dodges in for Donut, Group 2 stands on bosses hitbox and will be safe from point-blank.

Repeat once.
